OBRAVEN is a coined name, drawing on Ukrainian obriy (horizon) and obrav (chose). The line beneath the wordmark is the horizon; the gold point below the V marks the moment of clarity. Together they say: See further. Decide better. It sounds rather at home in Ireland, too.

Measurement strategy, wind resource and energy yield analysis, and independent review for onshore and offshore projects worldwide.
