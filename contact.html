Discuss a project

Bring one live project question.

You do not need a finished scope. A useful first conversation starts with the project stage, the question ahead, what already exists, who needs to rely on the result, and the timing.

A useful brief covers

  1. Project and market

    Onshore or offshore, country, approximate size, and development stage.

  2. The question

    What you are trying to answer or decide, in your own words.

  3. What already exists

    Measurements, EYAs, models, layouts, turbine options, and reviews.

  4. Who will rely on the result

    Internal team, investment committee, investor, lender, OEM, or permitting authority.

  5. Timing

    Any deadline or project gate, and when the answer is needed.

Before you get in touch

Questions people ask first

Can an OBRAVEN energy yield assessment be used for financing?

OBRAVEN carries out full, independent wind resource and energy yield assessments, prepared to the standard a financing requires. Which assessment a lender relies on is the lender’s decision: some accept an independent specialist’s report, others appoint an adviser of their own. Where a lender appoints its own adviser, OBRAVEN acts on the owner’s side: prepares the evidence, helps define and procure that assessment, and reviews the result. Any reliance on OBRAVEN’s work by a lender is agreed in writing.

Do you work on solar, storage, or hybrid projects?

Wind is OBRAVEN’s deepest technical base. On solar, storage, and hybrid projects, OBRAVEN reviews resource, yield, and performance assumptions and their business-case consequences. It does not do detailed equipment design.

Is a retainer needed?

No. A first engagement can be one defined assignment, with the scope, output, and fee basis agreed in advance. Call-off support, one question at a time, can follow where retained project context helps.

What does a first assignment cost?

It depends on the question, the data available, and the output needed. The proposal states the scope, the fee basis – fixed or capped where the scope allows – and the timing before any work starts.

Do you supply or install measurement equipment?

No. OBRAVEN sells no equipment and installs none. Acting for the client, it can manage a campaign from start to finish: set the strategy and the specification, shortlist contractors, oversee deployment, verify the installation on site, and review the data as it arrives. The measurement contractor is appointed by the client and contracts with the client; supply, installation, and field maintenance stay with that contractor.

Can OBRAVEN work inside our scope as a consultancy or owner’s engineer?

Yes. OBRAVEN can own a defined wind-analysis module inside a partner’s assignment, contracted through the partner or directly, with its role and boundaries stated in the scope.

How is confidential project material handled?

An NDA is put in place before confidential material is shared where needed. Material is used only for the engagement, and nothing that identifies a client or project is published without written permission.
